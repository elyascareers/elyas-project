# 06 Act

## Recommendations
1. Create marketing messages that highlight the value of membership for riders who take longer trips, since casual riders already ride for longer durations.
2. Target casual riders with weekend-focused promotions or offers, because they show higher activity on weekends.
3. Use popular stations among casual riders as locations for targeted campaigns or information about membership benefits.
4. Consider membership options that feel flexible for people who currently ride mainly on weekends or for leisure.

## Expected outcome
The marketing team would have a clearer way to reach casual riders with messages that match how they already use the service. This can help increase the number of annual members.
