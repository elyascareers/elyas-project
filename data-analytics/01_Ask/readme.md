# 01 Ask

**Business task:** Understand how annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently, so the marketing team can design a strategy to convert casual riders into members.

**Stakeholders**
- Lily Moreno, Director of Marketing
- Cyclistic marketing analytics team
- Cyclistic executive team

**Guiding questions**
1. How does ride duration differ between members and casual riders?
2. At what times of day do the two groups ride?
3. Which stations are most popular for each group?
4. How does usage differ across the days of the week?

**Scope:** Q1 2019 and Q1 2020 trip data.
