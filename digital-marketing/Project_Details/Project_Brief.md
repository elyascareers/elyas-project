# Project Brief — Customer Segmentation for Campaign Targeting

## Scenario
You are a junior marketing analyst at a retail/e-commerce company. The company runs
promotional campaigns, but only about 15% of customers respond, so most of the marketing
budget is spent reaching people who don't buy. The head of marketing wants to stop
blasting everyone and instead **target the customers most likely to convert**.

## Your task
Use the customer database to:
1. Segment customers based on their buying behaviour (RFM: Recency, Frequency, Monetary).
2. Identify which segments actually respond to campaigns.
3. Recommend where the marketing team should focus its budget.

## Deliverables
1. A clear statement of the business task.
2. A description of the data used.
3. Documentation of the cleaning and transformation.
4. A summary of the analysis.
5. Supporting visualizations and key findings.
6. Top three recommendations.

## Data
`Data_Raw/marketing_campaign.csv` — 2,240 customers: demographics, two years of spending by
product category, purchases by channel, and responses to past campaigns. Tab-separated.
Public dataset (iFood/superstore customer-analysis case).

## Guiding questions
- What does a high-value customer look like?
- Which segments respond to campaigns, and which don't?
- Which products and channels matter most?
- Where would a smaller, smarter campaign spend beat a blanket one?
