# Customer Segmentation & Campaign Targeting (Power BI)

Portfolio project #2 — **Digital Marketing / E-commerce**. Customer **RFM segmentation** on
a marketing-campaign dataset, done end-to-end in **Power BI**, to help a marketing team
target the right customers and lift campaign response.

**Question:** How do we segment customers so marketing can target the right people?

## TL;DR findings
- **Champions** (24% of customers) respond to campaigns at **30%**; **Hibernating** (6%) at **1%**.
- Response rate tracks RFM strength almost perfectly — a small group drives most responses.
- **Wine & meat** dominate spend; the physical **store** is the top purchase channel.
- One past campaign (Cmp2) flopped at **1.4%** acceptance.

## Recommendations
1. Focus campaign budget on Champions + Loyal Customers.
2. Run a win-back offer for "At Risk" customers.
3. Lead with wine/meat; don't repeat the Campaign-2 format.
