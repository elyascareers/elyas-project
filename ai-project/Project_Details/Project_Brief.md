# Project Brief &mdash; Applied / Agentic AI Showcase

## Purpose
Demonstrate practical, hands-on AI fluency: not just using AI to answer questions, but
**directing AI agents** to research, build, test, ship, and operate real products and
content workflows &mdash; with the governance that keeps the output safe and honest.

## What this project shows
1. **Agentic product development** &mdash; a repeatable, governed build pipeline and the
   owned products shipped with it (StockSafe, SafeRent, Zetranova Finance Portal,
   SocialManager, Stitchbook, a School Management System, and the Zetranova landing site).
2. **Agentic content automation** &mdash; a scheduled multi-agent pipeline that researches,
   drafts (with a human approval gate), and publishes content weekly.
3. **Supporting AI competency** &mdash; custom Claude skills created, connectors/MCPs used,
   prompt engineering, and AI governance.

## Deliverables
- A case-study report (`Final_Output/AI_Report.md`).
- A methodology / how-to guide so the approach can be reproduced (`Documentation/`).
- This brief.

## Boundaries (what's deliberately kept out)
- No client personal data and no internal client documents are published.
- Products shown are **owned** (Zetranova); internal build rules are summarised, not dumped.
- Nothing fabricated: only real, verifiable work is described.
