# Applied / Agentic AI (Portfolio Project #3)

Portfolio project #3 &mdash; **AI**. A showcase of hands-on, applied AI: directing AI agents
to research, build, test, ship, and operate real products and content workflows, under
rules that keep the output safe, honest, and accessible.

## What's here
- `Final_Output/AI_Report.md` &mdash; the case study (methodology, products, automation, competency map).
- `Documentation/AI_Methodology.md` &mdash; how to set this way of working up yourself.
- `Project_Details/Project_Brief.md` &mdash; the brief and boundaries.

## In one line
I don't just use AI &mdash; I **direct AI agents** to ship software and run automated
content pipelines, with human-in-the-loop gates before anything irreversible.

## Highlights
- A governed **Digital Product Builder** pipeline (intake &rarr; research &rarr; build &rarr; verify &rarr; handoff).
- Owned products built with it: **StockSafe, SafeRent, Zetranova Finance Portal, SocialManager, Stitchbook, a School Management System**.
- A **4-agent scheduled content pipeline** with a human approval gate and retry guardrails.
- Custom Claude **skills**, **connectors/MCPs**, prompt engineering, and AI **governance**.
