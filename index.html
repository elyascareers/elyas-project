1 · Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis

Google Data Analytics capstone · built end-to-end in Power BI

Question: How do annual members and casual riders use the bikes differently?

  • Members = 91.4% of rides, but short (~13 min) weekday trips → commuters.
  • Casual riders = 8.6%, but far longer (~23 min median) trips, mostly weekends → leisure/tourists.
  • Casual riding peaks at lakefront/tourist stations and grows into spring.
